# Creature-art QA references

These are editorial identification references for creature-art review. They support morphology and identity review only; they do not define rarity or encounter frequency.

## Reference authorities

- Smithsonian Tropical Research Institute (STRI), Shorefishes of the Greater Caribbean — primary species-level identity reference for Caribbean reef fishes: https://biogeodb.stri.si.edu/caribbean/en/thefishes/
- Florida Museum, Discover Fishes — supporting Atlantic/Caribbean species accounts: https://www.floridamuseum.ufl.edu/discover-fish/species-profiles/
- NOAA Fisheries — sea-turtle and queen-conch morphology references: https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/
- Smithsonian Ocean — echinoderm morphology context: https://ocean.si.edu/ocean-life/invertebrates/sea-stars
- FishBase — morphology and accepted-name cross-checks: https://www.fishbase.se/
- WoRMS / OBIS — marine taxonomic-name cross-checks: https://www.marinespecies.org/ and https://obis.org/

For locally authoritative plausibility, the source IDs and provenance embedded in `content/mexican-caribbean` remain the repository authority. Art QA does not create new creature records or infer encounter frequency.

## Issue #33 — missing-HD diagnostic matrix

Use this matrix before generation and again at full-resolution and collection-card review. An attractive image that misses these cues is a remake, not a `keep`.

### Batch A — distinctive / high-value local species

- `splendid-toadfish` — *Sanopus splendidus* — black head with white zebra-like lines; grey body with faint pale mesh; dark fins with broad orange/yellow edges; many head/jaw barbels, but only the central 1–2 chin barbels branched. Reject generic toadfish. **Primary:** STRI.
- `lionfish` — *Pterois volitans* — red/white zebra striping; very long unbranched pectoral rays and dorsal spines; spots on soft dorsal, anal and caudal fins. Reject short-finned generic scorpionfish. **Primary:** Florida Museum / Smithsonian invasions reference.
- `loggerhead-sea-turtle` — *Caretta caretta* — conspicuously large head and powerful jaws; reddish-brown, slightly heart-shaped hard carapace; usually five pairs of costal scutes; pale yellow plastron. Reject green/hawksbill head and shell proportions. **Primary:** NOAA Fisheries.
- `honeycomb-cowfish` — *Acanthostracion polygonium* — thick oblong box body; dark hexagonal/honeycomb network outlined by pale lines; horn-like spine above each eye and spine at lower rear corner of carapace. **Primary:** STRI; accepted name cross-checked with WoRMS/OBIS/FishBase.
- `rock-beauty` — *Holacanthus tricolor* — adult front of body yellow; most of rear body plus dorsal/front anal fin black; tail entirely yellow; orange gill-cover/anal margin accents and blue iris accents. **Primary:** FishBase/STRI.
- `spotted-moray` — *Gymnothorax moringa* — pale white/yellow ground with many distinct small round overlapping dark brown-to-purplish spots; eel-like body. Reject green moray or large irregular blotches. **Primary:** FishBase/STRI.
- `black-grouper` — *Mycteroperca bonaci* — robust grouper form; rectangular dark-grey flank blotches; outer third of second dorsal, anal and caudal fins black. Reject uniformly black or Nassau-grouper-like barring. **Primary:** FishBase/STRI.

### Batch B — surgeonfish / parrotfish

- `doctorfish` — *Acanthurus chirurgus* — grey-brown compressed disc with roughly 8–12 long thin dark vertical bars; tail nearly straight/slightly concave with blunt tips and no narrow white rear edge. **Primary:** STRI.
- `ocean-surgeonfish` — *Acanthurus tractus* — relatively uniform grey-brown body without doctorfish bars; short yellow lines behind eye; concave tail with pointed tips and a narrow pale/whitish-yellow rear edge. **Primary:** STRI.
- `princess-parrotfish` — *Scarus taeniopterus* — prefer identifiable terminal phase: blue chest; blue dorsal with pink center; pink upper/lower tail edges; yellow stripe above pectoral. Do not substitute generic green parrotfish. **Primary:** STRI.
- `queen-parrotfish` — *Scarus vetula* — prefer identifiable terminal phase: blue around mouth; oblique blue stripe from eye toward mid-back; pink tail. Keep visually distinct from princess parrotfish. **Primary:** STRI.
- `redband-parrotfish` — *Sparisoma aurofrenatum* — prefer terminal phase: red iris and red stripe below eye; yellow-and-black spot above pectoral base; black tail tips. **Primary:** STRI.

### Batch C — grunts / snapper / wrasses

- `bluestriped-grunt` — *Haemulon sciurus* — yellow body with strong blue longitudinal stripes; caudal fin and soft dorsal dark/black. **Primary:** STRI.
- `french-grunt` — *Haemulon flavolineatum* — roughly 12 yellow stripes; lower-body stripes run diagonally; fins yellow. Do not leak the bluestriped-grunt black-fin treatment. **Primary:** STRI.
- `mutton-snapper` — *Lutjanus analis* — red iris and reddish fins; blue lines under eye; small dark spot above lateral line below soft dorsal. **Primary:** STRI.
- `hogfish` — *Lachnolaimus maximus* — deep oblong body, long pointed snout; first three dorsal spines very long and filamentous; large terminal males pale/white with dark forehead/nape and dark dorsal/tail-base markings. **Primary:** STRI.
- `bluehead-wrasse` — *Thalassoma bifasciatum* — prefer terminal phase: vivid blue head and green body separated by black and white vertical bars. **Primary:** STRI.
- `yellowhead-wrasse` — *Halichoeres garnoti* — prefer terminal phase: yellow head/front body with prominent dark T/bar marking under the dorsal-origin area; do not substitute bluehead or yellowcheek wrasse. **Primary:** STRI.

### Batch D — butterflyfish / damsels / smaller reef fish

- `banded-butterflyfish` — *Chaetodon striatus* — silver body with four broad black vertical bars; disc-like butterflyfish form. Reject foureye ocellus pattern. **Primary:** STRI.
- `foureye-butterflyfish` — *Chaetodon capistratus* — cream body with thin chevron lines and one large eye-like ocellus below the rear dorsal; short pointed snout. **Primary:** STRI.
- `bicolor-damselfish` — *Stegastes partitus* — use a documented adult morph, preferably black front half with white rear half; avoid unrelated blue/yellow damsel patterns. **Primary:** STRI.
- `yellowtail-damselfish` — *Microspathodon chrysurus* — adult brown body, bright yellow tail, blue spots concentrated on upper body; deep robust damsel form. **Primary:** STRI.
- `blue-chromis` — *Azurina cyanea* — dark electric-blue elongate oval; deeply forked pointed tail with black upper and lower margins. **Primary:** STRI.
- `sharpnose-puffer` — *Canthigaster rostrata* — long pointed snout; brown upper/yellowish lower body; thin blue lines around eye and across snout plus blue body spots; yellow tail with broad black upper/lower margins and pale central tail-base area. **Primary:** STRI.
- `balloonfish` — *Diodon holocanthus* — robust inflatable porcupinefish with numerous long erectile spines longer than the eye; dark bar through eye; broad dark body blotches with relatively few spots; fins unspotted and tail base without spines. **Primary:** STRI.
- `bar-jack` — *Caranx ruber* — silver/light-blue jack with strongly forked tail; blue stripe from snout along upper back then down the lower edge of the lower tail lobe, bordered above by a black stripe from beneath second dorsal to tail tip. **Primary:** STRI.

## Taxonomy note discovered during #33 preparation

The existing `honeycomb-cowfish` record used *Acanthostracion polygonius*. Current STRI, FishBase, WoRMS/OBIS and Eschmeyer-derived records use **Acanthostracion polygonium**; WoRMS/OBIS explicitly treat *polygonius* as unaccepted. The #33 preparation branch corrects the content record before source-art metadata is created.
